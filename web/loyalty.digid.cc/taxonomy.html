Sector Classification

Nine sectors classify each loyalty program by its primary industry vertical. Used for filtering, comparison, and benchmarking.

airline 40

  • Major carriers (Delta, United, Lufthansa, Emirates, Singapore Airlines, etc.)
  • Alliance programs (Star Alliance, oneworld, SkyTeam)
  • Low-cost carrier programs (Ryanair, easyJet, Southwest)

retail 39

  • Department stores (Nordstrom, Macy's, Harrods)
  • Supermarkets (Tesco Clubcard, Kroger, Woolworths)
  • E-commerce (Amazon Prime, Rakuten, Flipkart)
  • Fashion & lifestyle (Sephora, H&M, Zara)

dining 16

  • Restaurant chains (Starbucks, McDonald's, Tim Hortons)
  • Coffee shops (Costa, Pret A Manger, Luckin)
  • Food delivery (DoorDash DashPass, Uber One, Rappi)
  • Fast casual (Chipotle, Panera, Jollibee)

credit_card 15

  • Bank loyalty programs (Amex MR, Chase UR, Citi TYP)
  • Co-branded cards (Delta Amex, United Chase, Marriott Bonvoy)
  • Store cards (Target RedCard, Nordstrom Visa)

entertainment 15

  • Streaming (Netflix, Disney+, Spotify)
  • Gaming (PlayStation Stars, Xbox Rewards, Nintendo)
  • Theme parks (Disney Parks, Universal, Six Flags)
  • Sports (FIFA, Aston Martin F1, Audience Rewards)

hotel 14

  • Global chains (Marriott Bonvoy, Hilton Honors, IHG One)
  • Luxury groups (Hyatt, Four Seasons, Ritz-Carlton)
  • Boutique & regional (Accor Live Limitless, Small Luxury Hotels)

fuel 12

  • Oil majors (Shell, BP, ExxonMobil, Chevron)
  • Convenience stores (Circle K, Casey's, 7-Eleven)
  • Regional operators (Petro-Canada, Esso, ADNOC)

other 9

  • Telecom (T-Mobile Tuesdays, Safaricom Bonga)
  • Super-apps (Grab, Gojek, Rappi)
  • Financial services (Revolut, N26)
  • Travel & mobility (Uber One, Lyft)

car_rental 8

  • Global brands (Hertz Gold, Avis Preferred, Enterprise Plus)
  • Regional (Europcar, Sixt, Hertz Europe)
  • Airline partnerships (National, Dollar/Thrifty)

Regional Classification

Six geographic regions classify where a program primarily operates. global means the program operates across multiple regions without a single primary market.

RegionCountDefinition
north_america 67 United States, Canada, Mexico — the largest single market for loyalty programs
global 30 Multi-regional programs (Star Alliance, Marriott, Amazon Prime) with no single primary market
asia_pacific 22 East Asia, Southeast Asia, South Asia, Australia/NZ, Japan
europe 22 EU, UK, Switzerland, Scandinavia, Eastern Europe
middle_efrica 14 Middle East + Africa combined (Emirates, Etihad, Safaricom, ADNOC)
latin_america 13 Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, Chile, Argentina, Central America
Note: middle_efrica is a known typo — should be split into middle_east and africa, or renamed to mena. 14 records affected.

Parameter Taxonomy

77 comparison parameters across 10 categories. Each parameter defines one dimension for benchmarking loyalty programs.

membership_tiers 12

  • Tier Count — number of status levels
  • Tier Names — names of each tier
  • Tier Structure Type — flat, gradient, hybrid
  • Qualification Metric — spend, nights, points, segments
  • Qualification Thresholds per Tier
  • Qualification Window — calendar year, rolling
  • Status Period Length — 12 months, lifetime
  • Downgrade Policy — soft landing, hard reset
  • Status Grace / Extension Period
  • Tier Membership Distribution — % per tier
  • Top-Tier Exclusivity — invite-only, earned
  • Status Match Availability

program_economics 10

  • Revenue Model — how the program makes money
  • Membership Fee Structure — free, paid tier, subscription
  • Profitability Disclosure
  • Deferred Revenue Flow
  • Audit Risk Profile
  • Partner Revenue Sharing
  • KPI Measurement Set
  • Program Maturity Level — emerging, established, mature
  • Regulatory Constraints — GDPR, CCPA, etc.
  • Paid Membership Layer — Amazon Prime, Uber One

earning_structure 9

  • Base Earn Rate — points per $1 spent
  • Earn Currency — points, miles, tokens, stars
  • Earn Metric Basis — spend, nights, segments, activity
  • Bonus Multiplier Structure — tier bonuses, promo multipliers
  • Partner Earning Network — earn at partners
  • Promo Mechanics — limited-time offers, challenges
  • Welcome / Enrollment Bonus
  • Earn Caps and Exclusions
  • Gamification Elements — streaks, challenges, badges

point_economics 9

  • Expiration Policy — never, 12-24 months, activity-based
  • Devaluation History — past award chart changes
  • Earn-to-Burn Ratio — earning vs redemption rates
  • Breakage Rate — % of points never redeemed
  • Breakage Recognition Method — revenue recognition
  • Point Liability Balance — outstanding points value
  • Liability Sensitivity to Breakage
  • Points as Collateral / Financing
  • Breakage Reversal Risk

program_identity 8

  • Program Name
  • Operator Company
  • Parent Company
  • Sector
  • Primary Region
  • Launch Year
  • Program Archetype — points, miles, cashback, tier-based
  • Primary Objective — retention, acquisition, engagement

elite_perks 7

  • Lounge Access Benefit
  • Upgrade Policy — complimentary, confirmed, waitlist
  • Priority Boarding / Check-in / Security
  • Late Checkout / Guaranteed Availability
  • Dedicated Support — phone line, chat, concierge
  • Experiential / Event Rewards
  • Fee Waivers and Service Perks

redemption_options 7

  • Redemption Options — flights, hotels, cash, gift cards, merchandise
  • Transfer Partners — airline/hotel/hotel transfer ratios
  • Redemption Value per Point — cpp / cpm benchmarks
  • Minimum Redemption Threshold
  • Award Pricing Model — fixed, dynamic, zone-based, auction
  • Blackout Dates and Award Availability
  • Redemption Channels — online, app, phone, partner

program_scale 6

  • Enrolled Member Count
  • Active Member Count — engaged in last 12 months
  • Partner Network Size — # of earn/burn partners
  • Geographic Coverage — countries/regions served
  • Member Share of Volume — % of total revenue from members
  • Alliance / Network Scale

digital_presence 5

  • Mobile App Availability — iOS, Android, both
  • Website / Member Portal
  • API / Open Platform — partner integrations
  • Digital Wallet Integration — Apple/Google Wallet passes
  • Personalization and Data Capability

co_branded_partnerships 4

  • Co-branded Credit Cards — bank + airline/hotel cards
  • Alliance / Coalition Membership — Star Alliance, oneworld
  • Cross-Industry Partnerships — earn at non-sector partners
  • Transfer-In Partners — convert external points into program
